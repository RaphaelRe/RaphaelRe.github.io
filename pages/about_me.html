About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair for AI in Healthcare and Medicine (Daniel Rückert) at TUM. My work revolves around Bayesian approaches and uncertainty quantification in the medical context.

What I am interested in

01

Bayesian neural networks

Inference algorithms for Bayesian neural networks, from sampling-based to variational approaches.

02

Uncertain data

Data that is uncertain itself: measurement error, noisy records, or sources that contradict each other.

03

Priors and inductive bias

Sensible definition and use of priors, and (soft) inductive bias through a Bayesian lens. Prior-fitted networks and tabular foundation models bring a lot of these topics together.

04

Other topics

My path left me with a huge interest in some other topics: differential privacy, federated learning, (financial) econometrics and time series. I am always happy to have a chat about theses topics!

Background

I did my PhD (Dr. rer. nat.) at the Department of Statistics at LMU Munich. During that time, I worked as a researcher and data scientist at the Institute for Medical Information Processing, Biometry, and Epidemiology.

My research there focused on Bayesian approaches to model uncertainty, for example in infectious disease modelling and with complex measurement error. I also worked on federated learning, specifically on validating models without access to the data.

Many aspects of this work are summarized in my thesis, Addressing uncertainty and complex data structures through Bayesian and classical approaches (LMU, 2025).
